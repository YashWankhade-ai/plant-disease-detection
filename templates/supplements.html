Agricultural Supplements & Crop Remedies

Curated guide of recommended fungicides, biological inoculants, and foliar fertilizers designed to cure and prevent foliar crop diseases.

Organic Bio-Pesticide Eco-Certified
Pure Cold-Pressed Neem Oil (10,000 ppm)

Botanical broad-spectrum antifungal and insect repelling emulsion.

Target Diseases: Powdery mildew, early leaf spots, rusts, whiteflies, aphids.

Dosage: 5 ml/L of water with 2 ml mild liquid soap as emulsifier.

Harvest Interval: 0 days (safe until harvest date).

Broad Spectrum Fungicide Preventative
Copper Oxychloride 50% WP

Inorganic copper protectant active against both fungi and bacterial spots.

Target Diseases: Tomato Bacterial Spot, Early & Late Blight, Apple Scab, Grape Black Rot.

Dosage: 2.5 - 3.0 g/L foliar spray, ensuring full leaf coverage.

Harvest Interval: 7 - 14 days before harvest.

Microbial Bio-Inoculant Probiotic
Bacillus Subtilis Microbial Spray

Beneficial antagonistic bacteria that colonize leaf surfaces and secrete antifungal lipopeptides.

Target Diseases: Tomato Leaf Mold, Septoria Spot, Corn Leaf Blight, Root rots.

Dosage: 4 - 5 g/L applied bi-weekly during humid conditions.

Harvest Interval: 0 days (safe for organic produce).

Multi-Site Protectant Chemical Control
Mancozeb 75% WP (Dithiocarbamate)

Standard contact agricultural fungicide providing protective barrier against spore germination.

Target Diseases: Potato Early & Late Blight, Tomato Early Blight, Grape Black Rot, Apple Scab.

Dosage: 2.0 - 2.5 g/L applied before rain or upon initial disease warnings.

Harvest Interval: 7 days. Wear protective mask and gloves.

Cell Wall Strengthener Nutrient Booster
Chelated Calcium + Boron (CaB)

Foliar liquid mineral booster that thickens plant cuticle and prevents blossom end rot.

Target Crops: Tomatoes, Apples, Peppers, Potatoes, Grape vines.

Dosage: 2.0 ml/L applied during early flowering and fruit development.

Harvest Interval: 0 days. Non-toxic plant nutrient.

Systemic + Contact Curative Action
Metalaxyl-M + Mancozeb

Powerful systemic fungicide that penetrates vascular tissue to arrest virulent oomycete infections.

Target Diseases: Potato Late Blight, Tomato Late Blight, Downy Mildew.

Dosage: 2.5 g/L as an emergency curative spray at first symptom detection.

Harvest Interval: 14 days before harvest.

Organic Biostimulant Natural Immunity
Liquid Norwegian Kelp Extract

Rich in auxins, cytokinins, and trace minerals to stimulate phytoalexin disease resistance.

Target Crops: All crops (Corn, Tomato, Apple, Grape, Potato).

Dosage: 3 ml/L foliar spray every 10-14 days.

Harvest Interval: 0 days. Enhances fruit color and sweetness.

Systemic SAR Inducer Foliar Feed
Potassium Phosphite (K-Phite)

Induces Systemic Acquired Resistance (SAR) to trigger natural chemical defenses against fungi.

Target Diseases: Downy mildew, Phytophthora, Alternaria blights.

Dosage: 2.5 ml/L foliar drench.

Harvest Interval: 1 day.

Antagonistic Fungi Rhizosphere Shield
Trichoderma Viride Spore Formulation

Parasitizes pathogenic fungi in the soil and foliage, releasing cell-wall degrading chitinase enzymes.

Target Diseases: Rhizoctonia, Fusarium, Pythium, Sclerotinia damping off.

Dosage: 5 g/L for root drench or 3 g/L for foliar application.

Harvest Interval: 0 days (100% bio-safe).

Pesticide Safety & Application Precaution:

Always wear personal protective equipment (gloves, eye protection, respirator) when spraying chemical fungicides. Spray during calm early mornings or late afternoons to avoid scorching leaves and harming pollinators. Strictly adhere to regional harvest interval laws.
